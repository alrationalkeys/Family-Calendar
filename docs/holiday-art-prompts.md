# Holiday art prompts

Prompts for the free image generator, one holiday per section. Each holiday needs up to three
images. The **frame** is the only one that matters; the sign and the sprite are extras.

Rules that make install painless (same as the Christmas, Thanksgiving and Birthday art):

- Frames: ask for 16:9 (1920 × 1080 if the tool lets you pick). The **centre must be one flat
  colour** with no gradient, glow or texture, so it keys out cleanly. Decorations stay in the outer
  15% of each edge, because the calendar covers the middle.
- Countdown signs: 8:3 landscape, empty in the middle, on a flat background colour.
- Sprites (things that fly or walk across): side view, on a flat background colour, not touching
  the edges. Use bright green `#1B9131` unless the art itself is green; then use magenta `#FF00FF`.
- Send each finished image as its own message so it saves as a file.

When the art comes in, Claude keys it, fits it, and adds the holiday to the automatic schedule.

**Status (Nov 2026): all six installed** — frames, countdown signs and flyers/runners are live in `static/skins/`.

| Holiday | Wall shows it | Flying/walking extra |
|---|---|---|
| New Year's Eve | Dec 31 – Jan 1 | Champagne cork popping across |
| Valentine's Day | Feb 1 – Feb 14 | Cupid flying across |
| St. Patrick's Day | Mar 10 – Mar 17 | Leprechaun running across |
| Easter | 2 weeks before Easter – Easter Sunday | Bunny hopping across |
| 4th of July | Jun 28 – Jul 4 | Bald eagle flying across |
| Halloween | Oct 1 – Oct 31 | Witch on a broom flying across |

Optional smaller ones that work with the same frame style: Mother's Day, Father's Day, your
anniversary, first day of school.
