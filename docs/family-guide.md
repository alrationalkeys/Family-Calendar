# FamilyHub — how to use it

Our family calendar on the wall. No app to buy, no monthly fee. Here's everything.

## The buttons along the bottom
Tap these to switch what you're looking at:

- **📅 Calendar** — everyone's schedule. Tap a day to see it close up. Tap **+ Event** to add something.
- **🏠 Home** — the lights, TVs and alarm (once that's set up).
- **⭐ Chores** — the kids' chores and their star scores. Tap a chore to check it off (you'll get a little celebration!).
- **📝 Lists** — the grocery list and the to-do list. Tap **+** to add, type it, hit enter.
- **📷 Photos** — our photos. They also float across the screen as a slideshow when it's been sitting idle.

## Adding things is easy
- **On the wall:** tap **+** on any list, or **+ Event** on the calendar, and type.
- **From your phone:** open the FamilyHub page on your phone (on our home WiFi) and add events, lists, chores, or photos from there. Anything you add shows up on the wall within a few seconds.
- **The magic box:** the phone page has a "quick add" box — just type normally, like *"Soccer Saturday 9am"* or *"we need milk and eggs"*, and it files it in the right place for you.

## Moving it: wall ↔ kitchen counter
It lifts right off — no tools:
1. **Lift it straight up** off the wall bracket.
2. **Set it into the counter stand** (it just drops in and leans back).
3. To put it back, lift it out of the stand and **hook it onto the wall bracket** again.
Takes about five seconds. The one cable tucks behind.

## If it freezes or looks stuck
1. Give it a moment — it might just be loading.
2. Tap the **gear icon** (top right) → **"↻ Reload the display."** That fixes most hiccups.
3. Still stuck? **Unplug the power for 10 seconds, then plug it back in.** It starts
   itself back up in under a minute. Nothing gets lost — everything's saved.

## Good to know
- It **dims/turns off at night** on a schedule and **wakes when you tap it** (or walk up, once the sensor's added).
- Each person has their **own color** so you can tell at a glance whose event is whose.
- It keeps working even if the **internet goes down** — the calendar and lists are saved right on it.

That's it. Tap around — you can't break it. 💛
