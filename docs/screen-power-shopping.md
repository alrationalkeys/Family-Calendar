# Wall screen power — shopping list

Recessed outlet behind the screen, fed from the 3-switch box on the other side of the wall.
Assumes you own nothing; skip what you already have. Prices are rough (Nov 2026).

## Electrical aisle
- [ ] **Leviton 689-W 15 A recessed duplex outlet** (Home Depot, model R52-00689-00W) — ~$13
- [ ] **1-gang "old work" box** (Carlon or similar) for it — ~$2–3
      (Alternative: Legrand TV1WTVSSWCC2 recessed media box with surge outlet, ~$32 — bigger; mounted sideways it
       must be under ~6 in tall to hide under the cleat. Lowe's Sanus 3-3/4 × 8.75 kit works mounted sideways too.)
- [ ] **14/2 NM-B cable** (white jacket), smallest coil or ~5 ft cut — ~$10–30
      ⚠ If the breaker for that switch says **20**, buy **12/2 (yellow jacket)** instead.
- [ ] **Wago 221 lever connectors**, pack with **5-port (221-415)** — ~$10–15
- [ ] **Plug-in outlet tester** (3 lights) — checks the new outlet is wired right — ~$10
- [ ] **Non-contact voltage tester** (Klein NCVT-1) — ~$20

## Cords
- [ ] **2× 1 ft flat-plug extension cords** — needed with the Leviton (keeps the plugs flat against the wall) — ~$8–12

## Tools
- [ ] Stud finder with live-wire (AC) detection — ~$25–50
- [ ] Drywall jab saw — ~$10
- [ ] Wire stripper (Klein 11055) — ~$15–20
- [ ] Fish sticks / glow rods (small kit) — ~$15–25
- [ ] 4-in-1 screwdriver — ~$10
- [ ] Drill + bits (a ½" bit for SnapToggles, small bit for pilot holes) — if you don't own a drill, ~$60+
- [ ] Headlamp or flashlight (the lights are off while you work) — ~$10
- [ ] Tape measure, small level, pencil, painter's tape, utility knife — ~$20

## Hanging the screen
- [ ] **#8 × 2½" wood screws** (4) — for a stud — ~$5
- [ ] **TOGGLER SnapToggle BA anchors** (2–4) — if no stud behind the cleat — ~$10–15
- [ ] **Velcro cable ties** + a few **Velcro/Command strips** for the power bricks — ~$10

**Total:** roughly $150–200 buying everything (most of that is tools); ~$45–60 for just the parts.

## Placement (from the chat)
- Screen centre ~55 in off the floor.
- Leviton box centred left-right on that mark, its middle ~1 in **below** it (behind the lower half of the back case, under the cleat).
- Breaker OFF and voltage-tester checked before opening the switch box. Add the new black to the
  **always-hot bundle** (not a wire going to a light), white to the neutral bundle, bare to ground.
  Not sure which is which → photo to Claude, or an electrician (~$150–250, under an hour).
- The 3D-printed mount stays the same; the stand piece isn't used on the wall.
