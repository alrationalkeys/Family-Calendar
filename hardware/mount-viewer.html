How it hangs
The case bolts to the screen's VESA holes. The cleat on the top of the case hooks over the wall cleat; the foot bar at the bottom is the same thickness so the display hangs plumb. Lift straight up to take it off the wall and drop it onto the stand's tongue.

Order of assembly

  1. Bolt back case to the screen's housing: 4 × M4 through the floor into the VESA holes.
  2. Drop the C3 in, HDMI and power face toward the bottom opening.
  3. Screw the cleat (case half) to the two top bosses and the foot bar to the two bottom bosses: 4 × M3 × 16.
  4. Screw the cleat (wall half) to the wall, bevel facing out and sloping down toward the wall. Two screws into studs or anchors.
  5. Hang the display. For the counter, hang it on the stand the same way.
